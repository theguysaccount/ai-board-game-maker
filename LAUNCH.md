# Launch

Machine-driven mode: nothing is posted, sent or messaged by anyone. Distribution runs through search and the product's own loops.

## Live

- https://theguysaccount.github.io/ai-board-game-maker/
- Built-in distribution: a small site link on every printed page and card (removable), "print it again for free" on every rules page, share links that reopen a game for a colleague, 48 search pages with titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, share cards and structured data (WebApplication, FAQPage, Article, BreadcrumbList, LearningResource), a sitemap, and IndexNow pings on every deploy.

## Production deploy steps

See OPERATIONS.md, key 1 (DNS records and commands), after a domain is chosen: printablegamemaker.com, playcampground.com (owned) or printablegames.app.

## Search terms the pages answer

- "printable [topic] bingo" (e.g. Spanish greetings, planets, Christmas, baby shower) → /topics/…
- "bingo card generator with clues", "custom bingo cards printable" → /games/bingo/ and /make/
- "I have who has cards [topic]" → /games/loop/ and topics
- "printable board game template", "make your own board game printable" → /games/summit/, /how-to-make-a-board-game/
- "memory matching cards printable [topic]" → /games/match/
- "online bingo caller", "memory game vs computer" → play pages (linked from games and topics)
- "classroom review games", "quiz night printable answer sheet" → /games/quiz/

## Human-only options (not done, by design)

- Google Search Console verification and sitemap submission.
- Teacher marketplaces, newsletters or forum posts.
- The print-on-demand storefront (OPERATIONS.md, key 2).
